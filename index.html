Our Mission

We're collecting gently used clothing to support foster youth in Sacramento — helping them feel confident, empowered, and seen.

Why It Matters

Foster youth often move homes with just a bag of clothes — or none at all. You can give them a fresh start, a little comfort, and a way to express themselves.

What We Collect

  • 👖 Jeans, pants, shorts
  • 👕 T-shirts, casual tops
  • 🧥 Sweatshirts, jackets
  • 👗 Dresses
  • 👟 Clean shoes
  • 🧢 Accessories (hats, belts, bags)

Must be clean and gently used. No undergarments, socks, or damaged items.

How You Can Help

🏫 Schools

  • Host a donation bin
  • Share in newsletters or announcements
  • Encourage student clubs to participate

🏪 Businesses

  • Host a donation bin
  • Donate unsold teen items
  • Sponsor supplies (bags, hangers, hygiene kits)

🙋 Volunteers

  • Sort and organize donations
  • Help distribute outfit bundles
  • Spread awareness on social media
